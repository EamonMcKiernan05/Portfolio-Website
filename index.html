  • Statistics hub on Swift Charts: per-exercise volume and estimated one-rep-max trends, personal records per rep range, seasonal bests and session records
Sync Service
  • lift-sync: a FastAPI service that upserts finished sessions into a single flat CSV — bearer auth, a single-writer lock, upsert by session ID, delete tombstones, atomic file rewrite, JSONL audit log and a health endpoint; 55 passing pytest tests, including two simultaneous uploads and an eight-thread upsert race
  • Ships as one Docker image with a compose file, and the CSV header is frozen so the file stays legible to a human and a model alike
  • Offline-first client: an outbox queues finished sessions and retries with backoff, tombstones deletions and guarantees a single upload per session — sync is optional and off by default, the phone stays the source of truth

  • One-time importer over four years of the old app's export (6,524 set rows, 636 sessions, 88 exercises, 11 categories) that lifts RPE out of the free-text notes where it had been buried as bare numbers and ranges, and rewrites it as a real column

  • Accessibility and Dynamic Type treated as evidence rather than assertion: the audit runs as its own test class at the largest accessibility text size, its 33 findings (contrast, hit areas under 44 pt, clipped labels) are logged as a known polish list instead of hidden, and everything a simulator cannot prove — HealthKit writes, Live Activities on a real lock screen, haptics — is listed as unverified rather than claimed
