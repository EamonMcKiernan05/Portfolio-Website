The prompt
Prompt captured from DeepSeek Harness session 61dd0d47, one shot, no follow-ups.
Create one complete, self-contained HTML file for THE CASTLE, a photorealistic real-time 3D TINY WORLD in three.js: a stone castle and its courtyard on a chunk of land floating on a plain white page, in warm late-afternoon sun, as a self-running scene with no interaction.

THE FRAMING, the tiny world:
- One irregular, organic CHUNK OF LAND floats in the middle of a plain WHITE page (#ffffff), spanning about 75 percent of the frame width, with plenty of white around it.
- Below the ragged grassy edge, the torn-out underside shows a realistic SOIL CROSS-SECTION tapering to a rough point: dark topsoil laced with fine grass roots, lighter subsoil, a band of grey clay, embedded pebbles, and a few roots sticking out of the sides.
- Small clumps of dirt and root tendrils dangle beneath. Now and then a tiny crumb breaks off, falls into the white and fades.
- The island bobs very gently, as if floating.
- The camera is a FIXED ORTHOGRAPHIC ISOMETRIC view, about 30 degrees down and 45 degrees around. It never moves, zooms or shakes; all the life is inside the scene.
- The edge of the island dissolves softly into the white.

THE LOOK: PHOTOREALISTIC, at full resolution.
- No pixelation and no dithering: realism changes the rendering, never the framing.
- Physically based limestone and granite blocks, mortar, slate and timber roofs, weathered oak, iron, cloth banners, grass, soil and water.
- One low warm sun from the side with real soft shadows and contact shadows, and a soft sky fill so no shadow is ever black.
- Subtle ambient occlusion, ACES filmic tone mapping and gentle bloom.
- A very subtle fixed tilt-shift blur at the island's far and near ends, so it reads as an exquisite miniature.
- No low-poly, flat shading, plastic surfaces or stained-glass look.

THE CASTLE is a real, convincing medieval stone castle filling the island:
- A tall square KEEP with a crenellated parapet, small arrow-slit windows and a steep slate roof on its corner turret.
- A curtain WALL with a wall-walk, merlons and two round towers with conical slate roofs, enclosing a cobbled COURTYARD.
- A GATEHOUSE with a pointed arch, a raised portcullis and a timber drawbridge over a short moat that runs out to the island's edge.
- Real weathering: weathered stone, moss in the joints, ivy on the north wall, rust on the ironwork, and cloth BANNERS that ripple on the towers.
- Every part is physically connected and supported.

THE COURTYARD AND ITS LIFE:
- A stone well, a timber stable with hay, a smithy with a glowing forge, a few barrels and crates, a small kitchen garden and one old oak tree.
- Tiny PEOPLE go about their day: a blacksmith hammering at the anvil, a guard pacing the wall-walk, a maid carrying a basket, a stable boy leading a horse, a cook at the kitchen door.
- Smoke curls from the chimney and the forge, a few chickens peck in the yard and a couple of crows circle the keep.
- The moat water ripples and reflects the sky.
- Only medieval things: no cars, wires or modern objects.

THE LOOP: one seamless loop of about 24 seconds. Every motion is built from the loop time, so the last frame flows straight into the first with no jump.
- The people walk their routes and return to where they began.
- The banners ripple, the smoke drifts and the forge glows.
- The moat water ripples, the crows circle, and the island bobs.
- Shadows agree with the sun.
- Nothing floats off the island except the falling crumbs.

Make it fill the window, resize cleanly, run smoothly at 60 fps and start with zero console errors. Draw everything in code; never fetch or hotlink an image, texture or model. Deliver it as a single self-contained HTML file; any libraries are allowed.

Add sound: a gentle original music track that suits the scene plus ambient sound and sound effects for the action, all synthesized in code, with a small 'click for sound' hint, starting on the first click. Log audio events to the console to confirm they fire, since you can't hear them.
